04 — HOW WE WORK

A short, boring, repeatable path to an agent you can defend.

01

Threat model

What the agent can touch, who it acts as, and what the worst case looks like. Written down before anything is built.

02

Design the boundary

Identity, least-privilege tools, approval points and audit. The unglamorous decisions that decide everything later.

03

Build with evidence

Durable workflows, evals in CI and traces from the first commit, so every claim about the agent has a number behind it.

04

Attack it first

Red-team the agent before production does: injected instructions, poisoned tools, runaway loops. Fix what breaks, then re-run.

05

Hand over clean

Docs, runbooks and the measurements your risk and audit teams will ask for, in the shape they will ask for them.
